# Launch films and launch pictures

launch-film makes a film of {fact:launch-film.length_min} to {fact:launch-film.length_max} seconds from a product's own site. It reads the brand and the page's words, writes the plan as `film.json` and renders `film.mp4` with the look's own music and sound effects. `plan_launch_film` writes the plan, `render_launch_film` renders it, and `make_launch_film` does both in one job. `make_launch_stills` makes the launch pictures from the same plan.
<!-- with check_video -->
`check_video` measures the film (the checks topic).
<!-- end -->
Nothing is generated by AI: every word on screen comes from the page or from the user.

## Decide first

- Plan first: `plan_launch_film` returns the words before anything renders, so a wrong name costs no render. `make_launch_film` suits a quick film when the user has no wishes about its words.
- `url` is the product's website, or its App Store or Google Play listing, whose icon, screenshots and rating are used with the site it names. A social profile is refused, and so is a host without a dot, like localhost.
<!-- local -->
  A site on this machine works as http://127.0.0.1:<port>, but the brand's name may then come from that address: check it in `film.json`.
<!-- end -->
- Ask for the site when the request has none. The rest has defaults: `aspect` {default:plan_launch_film.aspect}, `seconds` {default:plan_launch_film.seconds}, and a `look` picked from the brand.
- `look` sets the style and the tempo of the film's music, and so how long a bar lasts: launch {fact:launch-film.look_bpm.launch} BPM, aurora {fact:launch-film.look_bpm.aurora}, gridline {fact:launch-film.look_bpm.gridline}, pop {fact:launch-film.look_bpm.pop}, journal {fact:launch-film.look_bpm.journal}. `launch_film_guide` with `topic` looks says what each one suits:
```call
launch_film_guide {"topic": "looks"}
```
- `seconds` aims the plan at a length, {bounds:plan_launch_film.seconds}. Clips last whole bars and the plan never stretches one to fill the time, so the film can end several seconds away from it, often short. `film.timeline.json` has the real length.
- `aspect` is 16:9 or 9:16. For both, make a second plan with the other `aspect` and `words` set to the first plan's `words.json`, so both films say the same.
- The sound is the look's own bed and sound effects, made in code. `film.json` takes no track: a file named in its music field is ignored without a word. For the user's own track, plan with `music` false.
<!-- with fit_music -->
  The music topic fits the track to the film.
<!-- end -->
<!-- with easybrandkit -->
<!-- local -->
- `brand` takes the folder `make_brand_kit` saved, or its `brand.json`. Make the brand kit first when the user wants to see the logo and colors before the film; without `brand`, the kit reads the brand from the site itself.
<!-- end -->
<!-- hosted -->
- `brand` takes the `brand.json` of an earlier `make_brand_kit` job, as job:<job_id>/brand.json. Make the brand kit first when the user wants to see the logo and colors before the film; without `brand`, the kit reads the brand from the site itself.

## Steps

<!-- local -->
1. Plan into a folder of its own:
```call
plan_launch_film {"url": "https://linear.app", "out_dir": "launch/film-16x9"}
```
<!-- with easybrandkit -->
   With a brand kit, give `plan_launch_film` its folder as `brand`:
```call
plan_launch_film {"url": "https://linear.app", "brand": "launch/brand", "out_dir": "launch/film-16x9"}
```
<!-- end -->
2. Read the result, which holds `film.json`, `words.json` and `brand/BRAND.md`. Check the brand's name first: the kit takes it from the site's name, its title or its host, and the film shows it. Then check every word on screen against `words.json`, and look at `brand/card.png`. When the brand is wrong, as with a cookie wall, a login page or no logo, tell the user what came back and ask for the right page or the logo file. Never render a wrong brand: the film would show it as the product's.
3. Change `film.json` where it is wrong, as the film-json topic says.
4. Render it, which takes {fact:launch-film.render_minutes} for a 30 second film, then call `wait_for_job` with the `job_id` until it is done:
```call
render_launch_film {"film": "launch/film-16x9/film.json", "wait_seconds": 0}
```
5. Check the film as the checks topic says, and look at the `sheet.png` it makes:
```call
check_video {"film": "launch/film-16x9/film.mp4", "expect": "{fact:launch-film.size_16_9}@{fact:launch-film.fps}", "seconds": "{fact:launch-film.length_min}-{fact:launch-film.length_max}"}
```
6. Make the pictures from the same plan when the user wants them, and look at each one:
```call
make_launch_stills {"source": "launch/film-16x9/film.json", "for": ["ph", "og"]}
```

`make_launch_film` does steps 1 and 4 in one job and also saves frames/: read the words in its result the same way, and render again after a change.
```call
make_launch_film {"url": "https://linear.app", "aspect": "9:16", "out_dir": "launch/film-9x16", "wait_seconds": 0}
```

## The other parameters

- `brand`, on `make_launch_film` and `plan_launch_film`, takes a brand kit, which the kit copies into brand/ in the film's folder; without it, the kit reads the brand from the site. `words` takes a `words.json` from an earlier plan instead of reading the page again. `music` false leaves out the bed and `sfx` false the sound effects; the plan writes both into `film.json`.
- `render_launch_film` takes `film`, the `film.json`, and writes `film.mp4` and `film.timeline.json` next to it, replacing an earlier render there.
- `jobs` is the number of clips `make_launch_film` and `render_launch_film` render at once, {default:render_launch_film.jobs} unless given; 1 suits a slow machine.
- `make_launch_stills` takes `source`, a `film.json` or the site's address, and `for`, the places.
<!-- local -->
- `keep` keeps each clip's MP4 in a launch-film-work- folder in the system's temp folder, which the summary names.
- `out_dir` is the film's folder, launch-film-<host> unless given. Give each film its own: a second plan there replaces `film.json`, `words.json` and brand/ and leaves the old `film.mp4`, `film.timeline.json`, frames/ and stills/, which no longer match it; a render replaces `film.mp4` and `film.timeline.json`. Given a `film.json`, `make_launch_stills` saves next to it and takes no `out_dir`.
- Given `brand`, the kit deletes brand/ in `out_dir` and copies the kit in its place, and makersmcp does not start it while that brand/ holds a file of the user's the copy would not bring back. To use the kit already in brand/, give `brand` as `out_dir` with /brand added. Without `brand`, the kit makes brand/ again where it is, replacing a kit's own files and keeping the others, except for an App Store page, which gets a new brand/.
<!-- end -->

## What they write

| File | Made by | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| brand/ | plan, make, stills from an address | the brand kit the film uses: `brand/BRAND.md`, `brand/brand.json`, `brand/card.png`, the logo and screen files |
| `words.json` | plan, make | the page's title, description, headings and the line under each |
| `film.json` | plan, make | the plan, as the film-json topic says |
| `film.mp4` | make, render | the film, {fact:launch-film.fps} frames a second at {fact:launch-film.size_16_9} or {fact:launch-film.size_9_16}, H.264 with AAC sound at {fact:launch-film.sample_rate} Hz |
| `film.timeline.json` | make, render | the film's length and each clip's kind, type, start and length, in seconds |
| frames/ | make | two stills a clip and `frames/sheet.jpg`, for review; a render leaves them as they were |
| stills/ | `make_launch_stills` | the launch pictures and `stills/STILLS.md` |

frames/ is for review and stills/ for the places, though both are called stills. The sound is normalized to {fact:launch-film.lufs_target} LUFS under a {fact:launch-film.true_peak} dBTP ceiling before it is encoded, and a film measures about {fact:launch-film.lufs} LUFS, with a true peak that can pass {fact:launch-film.check_true_peak} dBTP. Rendering again changes neither.

## Launch pictures

`make_launch_stills` makes opaque PNGs at the exact size of each place:

| for | Files | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ph | `ph-1.png`, the logo, the promise and the screen, then up to {fact:launch-film.ph_frames_max} of the film's scenes from `ph-2.png` on | {fact:launch-film.still_size.ph} |
| ph | `ph-thumbnail.png` | {fact:launch-film.still_size.ph_thumbnail} |
| og | `og.png`, a shared link's preview | {fact:launch-film.still_size.og} |
| x | `x.png`, a post on X | {fact:launch-film.still_size.x} |
| play | `play-feature.png`, the Google Play feature graphic only | {fact:launch-film.still_size.play} |
| chrome | `chrome-small.png`, the logo alone, and `chrome-marquee.png`, the logo and the screen | {fact:launch-film.still_size.chrome_small}, {fact:launch-film.still_size.chrome_marquee} |

The words and the look come from `film.json`, so give `source` the film's `film.json` for pictures that match it; given the site, it plans a film first with the default look, length and aspect. No picture is 9:16, and an app's store screenshots are not among them: say so when a place needs one.
<!-- local -->
To change a picture's words, change `film.json` and make the pictures again.
<!-- end -->

## When something goes wrong

- `film.json has problems:` and a list: each line names a clip and a field, as the film-json topic explains.
<!-- local -->
  Fix each one and render again.
<!-- end -->
<!-- hosted -->
  A plan cannot be changed here: tell the user what each line says.
<!-- end -->
- `clip <n>: no logo could close the film: <reasons>`: no logo file of the brand kit could be used.
<!-- local -->
  Point the logo clip at another logo file or an app icon, as the film-json topic says, or ask the user for a logo file.
<!-- end -->
<!-- hosted -->
  Ask the user for a logo, since a plan cannot be changed here.
<!-- end -->
- `"<url>" is not a command or an address. Run launch-film --help.` from `make_launch_film`, `give the site's address, e.g. launch-film plan example.com` from `plan_launch_film`, and `"<url>" is not an address or a film.json` from `make_launch_stills`: the host has no dot, like localhost.
- `Not on Google Fonts, so the look's own typeface stood in: <family>.` is a note, not a failure: tell the user the film uses the look's typeface for that family.
<!-- with easybrandkit -->
- easybrandkit's own errors, like a bot check or a social profile, mean no brand was read: the brand topic has each one.
<!-- end -->
<!-- local -->
- `<tool> replaces the brand folder in <folder> with a copy of <kit>, deleting what is there, and <folder>/brand holds <files>, which its brand.json does not list and the copy would not bring back. Give another out_dir, or move those files first.` Nothing was deleted: give another `out_dir`, or give `brand` as that brand/ folder to use it where it is. The same holds for `<tool> makes the brand folder in <folder> again`, where a file of the user's has a name the kit writes.
- `<kit> is that same folder under another name, so it would be gone before the copy. Give brand as <folder>/brand to use it where it is.`
- `out_dir: with these arguments make_launch_stills saves in <folder> and cannot save anywhere else. Leave out_dir out.`
<!-- end -->

## Hand over

<!-- local -->
- The film: the path of its `film.mp4`, its length from `film.timeline.json`, its look and aspect, the check's verdict, and one line on each clip.
<!-- end -->
<!-- hosted -->
- The film: the link to its `film.mp4`, which works for {limit:keep_days} for anyone who holds it, its length from `film.timeline.json`, its look and aspect, the check's verdict, and one line on each clip.
<!-- end -->
- The pictures by place, with their sizes.
- What is left to the user: a track of their own under the film, and the upload to each place.
